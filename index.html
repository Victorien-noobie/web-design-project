Welcome

Welcome to Beaches Around Mauritius. This website is designed to help visitors discover the island's most beautiful beaches and plan enjoyable and safe beach visits.

Discover the Beauty of Mauritius

Mauritius is known for its crystal-clear lagoons, white sandy beaches, and breathtaking coastal views.

Featured Beaches

Le Morne Beach
Flic en Flac Beach

Flic en Flac is a popular west coast beach known for its long shoreline, coral reefs, and beautiful sunsets. It offers various water activities.

Grand Baie Beach

Grand Baie Beach is well known for its vibrant atmosphere, nearby restaurants, nightlife, and water sports activities.

Pereybere Beach

Pereybere Beach is a small but lively beach with clear waters,perfect for swimming and snorkeling. It is popular by both locals and tourists.

Mont Choisy Beach

Mont Choisy Beach is a long, curved beach in the north of Mauritius known for its soft white sand and calm, shallow waters. It is ideal for swimming, jogging, picnics, and enjoying peaceful sunsets.

Tamarin Beach

Tamarin Beach, located on the west coast, is famous for its dark sand and strong waves. It is a popular spot for surfing and dolphin watching, with a relaxed atmosphere and beautiful mountain views.

Belle Mare Beach

Belle Mare Beach is one of the longest beaches in Mauritius, stretching along the east coast. It is known for its clear turquoise water, gentle breeze, and peaceful environment, making it perfect for swimming and sunrise views.

Trou aux Biches Beach

Trou aux Biches Beach is a popular northern beach with crystal-clear water and fine white sand. It is well known for snorkeling, thanks to its coral reefs and rich marine life close to the shore.

Île aux Cerfs Beach

Île aux Cerfs Beach is located on a small island off the east coast of Mauritius. It is famous for its stunning lagoons, water sports, and postcard-perfect scenery, making it a favorite destination for tourists.

Where is Mauritius?

Mauritius is located in the Indian Ocean, east of Madagascar. The map shows the location of the island and its surrounding beaches.
