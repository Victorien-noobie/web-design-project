Welcome

This page is dedicated to providing you with essential safety tips for enjoying the beautiful beaches of Mauritius. Whether you're a local or a tourist, it's important to be aware of potential hazards and how to stay safe while having fun in the sun. From understanding ocean currents to protecting yourself from the sun, we've got you covered with practical advice to ensure a safe and enjoyable beach experience.

Swim Safely

Always swim in designated areas and go beyond the safety buoy. Be aware of ocean currents , wave conditions and also the weather conditions. AVOID SWIMMING IN DANGEROUS CONDITIONS SUCH AS CYCLONES OR GOING OUTSIDE IN RISK OF FLOODS.

Protect Yourself from the Sun

The sun in Mauritius can be intense, so it's crucial to protect your skin. Always apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen with a high SPF before heading to the beach, and reapply it every two hours or after swimming. Wear a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, and lightweight clothing to shield yourself from harmful UV rays.

Be Cautious of Marine Life

While the marine life in Mauritius is beautiful, it's important to be cautious. Avoid touching or disturbing marine animals, as some can be dangerous. If you encounter jellyfish, sea urchins, or other potentially harmful creatures, seek medical attention if stung or injured.

Water Activities

Follow instructions given by activity providers carefully. Wear safety equipment such as life jackets when required.

Children Safety

Always supervise children near the water. Make sure they stay close and wear flotation devices if needed.

Emergency Awareness

Know the local emergency numbers. If someone is in danger, alert lifeguards or authorities immediately.

General Emergency: 112 or 999 (covers police, ambulance, and fire services)

Fire services: 115

Ambulance (SAMU): 114.

Coastguards: 212 27 47

Tourism Police: 213 17 40

Child Protection: 113
